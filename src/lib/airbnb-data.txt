Ideal for longer stays and remote work, Casa Jirafa offers more comfort and value than a typical small 1BR, with 2 bedrooms, 2 baths, parking, a full kitchen, in-unit laundry, and room to spread out in walkable El Centro.
The space
Casa Jirafa is a comfortable Mexican-style condo in walkable El Centro that is especially well suited for longer stays and remote work. Compared with a typical small 1BR, you get more room to spread out, 2 bedrooms, 2 baths, onsite parking, a full kitchen, and in-unit laundry. Warm character, practical comfort, and great day-to-day livability make it easy to settle in for a week or a month.
Guest access
You’ll have full access to the condo, with the exception of a few locked owner storage cupboards.
Keys are provided by our onsite manager. Please coordinate your arrival time in advance so we can ensure a smooth check-in. The building is very secure, and keys are required for both entry and exit.
Sunday arrivals require advance notice, as it is our manager’s day off. We’re happy to accommodate — we just need to plan ahead.
Other things to note
We’re just a few blocks from the Malecón, with a steep stairway on the final stretch to the condo. Guests with mobility concerns may prefer Uber/taxi or consider a different location. If you enjoy daily exercise, the stairs offer great views along the way.
The pool is heated during winter months.
Fast 80/80 Mbps Wi-Fi is available, with optional Ethernet for a wired connection. Roku TVs are located in the living room and master bedroom.
Air conditioning is available in both bedrooms. Electricity costs are high locally, so we ask guests to turn off A/C and lights when not in use. Ceiling fans and cross-breezes (opening front and rear doors) keep the space comfortable most of the year.
Onsite covered parking and in-unit washer/dryer are included.
The gas grill behind the kitchen is available for guest use. Please do not use the charcoal grill on the property.
